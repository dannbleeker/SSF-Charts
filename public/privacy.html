SSF Charts — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

SSF Charts is an open-source Microsoft Office add-in. It ships in two parts: a PowerPoint add-in that turns chart definitions into native, editable PowerPoint shapes, and a smaller Excel companion ("SSF Charts Link") that turns the range you have selected into a chart configuration you can paste into PowerPoint. Both are designed to keep your data with you, and this policy covers both.

What data SSF Charts handles in PowerPoint

The chart data and settings you enter are processed entirely inside your PowerPoint client to draw shapes on your slide. SSF Charts persists a chart's configuration in a shape tag inside your own document (so a chart can be re-edited later) and — only when you press Save style to deck — a small style record in the presentation's own custom XML, so that a branded deck keeps its look when you send the file on.

On your device, in your browser's localStorage, it keeps your saved templates, your style defaults and small interface preferences. If you run the add-in's built-in diagnostics, a log of that run is kept in the same place, so that a run which crashes the pane can still be downloaded afterwards. None of it leaves your device.

What data SSF Charts handles in Excel

The Excel companion is a one-way bridge into PowerPoint. When you press Generate from selection it reads the values of the range you have selected, converts them into a chart configuration, and shows that configuration in the pane; Copy places it on your clipboard so you can paste it into SSF Charts in PowerPoint. All of that happens inside your Excel client. The companion does not write to your workbook and stores nothing on your device.

What we do not do

  • We do not transmit your slides, workbooks, chart data, or documents to any server we control.
  • We do not use accounts, logins, analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell or share any personal information — we don't collect any.

Hosting

The static files of both add-ins (scripts, icons, both task panes, this page) are served over HTTPS from ssf-chart.struktureretsundfornuft.dk, hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, the host may record standard technical request logs (e.g. IP address, timestamp) for security and reliability. SSF Charts itself adds no additional collection on top of that.

Besides loading its own files from that host, the PowerPoint pane makes one request of its own: when it opens it reads a small build.json from the same host, so it can tell you when the pane you have open is older than the deployed site. Nothing about you or your document is sent with it. Those files and Microsoft's Office.js library, described next, are the only network requests either add-in makes.

Third-party services

Inside PowerPoint and Excel, the add-in loads Microsoft's Office.js library from Microsoft's content delivery network, and runs within the Microsoft Office platform, which is governed by Microsoft's own privacy terms.
